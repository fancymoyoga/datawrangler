# High-Risk Patient Outreach Procedure

**Document ID:** CP-104 · **Version:** 3.0 · **Owner:** Care Management Operations · **Effective:** 2026-04-01
**SAMPLE DOCUMENT — fictional, created for a product demo. Not clinical guidance.**

## Purpose
Make sure care managers spend their limited time on the patients most likely to be readmitted, and that every high-risk patient gets timely, documented outreach.

## Risk tiers
Each discharged patient receives a 30-day readmission risk score between 0 and 1. Care managers use these tiers:

| Tier | Score | Outreach standard |
|---|---|---|
| High | 0.40 and above | Call within 24–48 hours; visit within 7 days; condition-specific protocol applies in full |
| Medium | 0.20 to 0.39 | Call within 72 hours; visit within 14 days |
| Low | Below 0.20 | Standard discharge instructions; automated text check-in at day 3 |

## Steps
1. **Review the daily high-risk list** each morning. Patients are sorted by risk score, highest first.
2. **Read the patient summary before calling:** diagnoses, recent admissions, missed appointments, and notes from previous outreach.
3. **Call the patient.** Confirm understanding of medications, follow-up appointments, and warning signs. Use the condition-specific protocol (CP-101 heart failure, CP-102 COPD, CP-103 diabetes).
4. **If the patient cannot be reached after two attempts:** try an alternate contact (caregiver or emergency contact), send a portal message, and notify the primary care provider. For High-tier patients, request a home visit or community health worker visit within 72 hours.
5. **Identify and act on barriers:** transportation, cost of medications, no caregiver, language needs. Refer to social work when needed.
6. **Document the outcome** in the patient engagement platform the same day.

## Staffing guideline
One care manager can complete about 12–15 High-tier outreach calls per day including documentation. Use the number of High-tier discharges expected this week to plan care manager coverage.

## Measures of success
- Percentage of High-tier patients reached within 48 hours (target: 90%).
- Percentage of High-tier patients with a 7-day visit (target: 80%).
- 30-day readmission rate by risk tier.
