# COPD Discharge and Transition-of-Care Protocol

**Document ID:** CP-102 · **Version:** 1.4 · **Owner:** Pulmonary Care Management · **Effective:** 2026-02-01
**SAMPLE DOCUMENT — fictional, created for a product demo. Not clinical guidance.**

## Purpose
Reduce return visits and readmissions for patients discharged after a COPD flare-up by making sure they can use their inhalers correctly, have an action plan, and are seen soon after discharge.

## Who this applies to
- Adult patients discharged with a primary diagnosis of COPD or a COPD exacerbation.
- **High** risk patients get every step below. **Medium** risk patients get steps 1, 2 and 3.

## Steps after discharge
1. **Inhaler check before discharge.** A nurse or respiratory therapist watches the patient use each inhaler and corrects their technique.
2. **Written COPD action plan.** The patient leaves with a plan that explains what to do on good days, on bad days, and in an emergency.
3. **Follow-up call within 48 hours** to confirm the patient has filled prescriptions (including any steroid or antibiotic course) and is using the action plan.
4. **Clinic visit within 7–14 days** with pulmonology or primary care.
5. **Pulmonary rehabilitation referral** for eligible patients, placed before discharge.
6. **Smoking cessation support** offered to every patient who smokes.

## When to escalate
Escalate to the on-call pulmonary nurse the same day if:
- Breathing is worse than usual and not improving with the action plan.
- The patient is using their rescue inhaler much more often than normal.
- The patient has not filled a prescription or cannot afford it.
- The patient cannot be reached after two attempts (follow CP-104, step 4).

## Documentation
Record inhaler technique results, action plan delivery, and every outreach attempt in the patient engagement platform.

## Measures of success
- Percentage of patients with a documented action plan at discharge (target: 95%).
- Percentage seen in clinic within 14 days (target: 75%).
- 30-day readmission rate for COPD patients.
