# Heart Failure Discharge and Transition-of-Care Protocol

**Document ID:** CP-101 · **Version:** 2.1 · **Owner:** Cardiology Care Management · **Effective:** 2026-01-15
**SAMPLE DOCUMENT — fictional, created for a product demo. Not clinical guidance.**

## Purpose
Reduce 30-day readmissions for patients discharged after a heart failure admission by making sure every patient has a follow-up plan, understands their medications, and knows the warning signs.

## Who this applies to
- Adult patients discharged with a primary or secondary diagnosis of heart failure.
- Patients flagged **High** readmission risk get every step below. **Medium** risk patients get steps 1, 2 and 4.

## Steps after discharge
1. **Follow-up call within 48 hours** of discharge by a care manager. Confirm the patient has their medications, understands the dosing, and has a scale at home.
2. **Clinic visit within 7 days** with cardiology or the primary care provider. Book it before the patient leaves the hospital when possible.
3. **Medication review within 72 hours** (see CP-105). Pay special attention to diuretics and blood pressure medications.
4. **Daily weight checks at home.** The patient records their weight every morning, before eating, and reports it through the patient portal or by phone.
5. **Diet guidance:** low-sodium diet and fluid guidance as prescribed by the provider.

## When to escalate
Escalate to the on-call cardiology nurse the same day if any of the following occur:
- Weight gain of more than 2 lb (about 1 kg) in one day or 5 lb (about 2 kg) in one week.
- New or worsening shortness of breath, swelling in the legs, or needing more pillows to sleep.
- The patient cannot be reached after **two** call attempts within 72 hours (follow CP-104, step 4).
- The patient reports they have not filled a new prescription.

## Documentation
Record every outreach attempt and its outcome in the patient engagement platform, including what was discussed and any barriers (transportation, cost of medications, caregiver support).

## Measures of success
- Percentage of patients contacted within 48 hours (target: 90%).
- Percentage with a clinic visit within 7 days (target: 80%).
- 30-day readmission rate for heart failure patients.
